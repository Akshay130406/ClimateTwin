# ClimateTwin

## AI-Powered Urban Climate Decision Engine

ClimateTwin is an AI-powered urban climate decision-support platform that helps cities:

- Predict climate risks
- Understand why areas are vulnerable
- Simulate future climate scenarios
- Identify vulnerable populations
- Simulate climate interventions
- Optimize climate action within a budget
- Evaluate long-term impacts of development projects
- Compare sustainable alternatives

## Core Workflow

MONITOR → PREDICT → EXPLAIN → SIMULATE → IDENTIFY → OPTIMIZE → DECIDE
